User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 27, 2026

NetEra processes Instagram message data only to provide inbound customer support auto-replies for connected Instagram business accounts.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data associated with your interaction with our app, email us at:

me@michaelemil.com

Please include:

  • Your Instagram username or the Instagram account you contacted
  • The approximate date of your message
  • A clear request to delete your data

What We Delete

We do not store Instagram message content long-term. Message content and sender IDs are processed in real time only to classify the inquiry and send a predefined reply.

If any temporary processing records or error logs exist, we will delete them within 30 days of receiving a valid deletion request.
